CardputerDict pronunciation

Place user-supplied MP3 or PCM WAV (8/16-bit, mono/stereo, 8..48kHz) in audio/en/.
Limit: 512KB/file, max 30 seconds. Filename: lowercase English headword; percent-encode spaces/apostrophes/periods.
Use tools/prepare_audio_pack.py to prepare file names, or tools/install_audio.py for USB installation.

Fn+P: speak/stop. Fn+W: configure 2.4GHz WiFi on device. Fn+= / Fn+-: volume.
The device checks audio/en/, then audio/cache/en/, then (if configured) downloads over verified HTTPS and caches on SD.
WiFi is off at boot and after download. User WiFi credentials stay in device NVS, not in this directory.
No third-party pronunciation recordings are included in the public data package.
